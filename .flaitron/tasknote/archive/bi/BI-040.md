  **No clarifications needed.** Explicit assumptions:

  1. **Enable `plugin:jsx-a11y/recommended` at error** (not Next's six-rule warn subset alone).
  2. **Direct pin** `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` matching the version Next already pulls (~6.10.x).
