**Discovery Notes:**

**The `getByText('Approved')` risk, checked before fixing anything:** `PROGRESS_BADGES` in `components/Sidebar.tsx` has three words — `undecided`, `kept`, `approved` — rendered as `sr-only` text `"{n} {word}"` (e.g. "1 approved") whenever that decision's count is > 0 for the task's visible round. `ReviewGrid.tsx`'s own card-badge labels are `'Approved'`, `'Kept'`, `'Discarded'` (Title Case) — no `'undecided'` badge on the card (undecided cards show no badge), and **no `'discarded'` entry in `PROGRESS_BADGES`**, so `Discarded` at `review-keyboard.spec.ts:36` was never at risk. `Kept` and `Approved` both are. In `review-keyboard.spec.ts`'s test, `getByText('Kept')` (line 31) throws before `getByText('Approved')` (line 41) ever runs — Playwright aborts a test at its first failed `expect` — so whether line 41 also collides was genuinely unknown until the line-31 fix let execution reach it. Fixed both proactively with `{ exact: true }` rather than fixing line 31 alone and finding out line 41 was still broken on the next run.
