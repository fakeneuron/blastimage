1. *Test-block comment names the deleted `draftKindRef`*: **note, fixed.** Comment-only edit in `Sidebar.test.tsx`, with no assertion touched. The Acceptance verify moved from "file untouched" to "comment-only diff", and the file was added to `touches:`.

7. *Two call sites repeat the input className*: **note, declined.** The duplication was there before; moving styling into the component goes beyond a behavior-preserving extraction. Left for the BI-067.N audit to weigh.
8. *No `SidebarDraftInput.test.tsx`*: **note, declined.** Acceptance pins the behavior through Sidebar (the user-visible surface), the 6/6 mutation receipt shows the component is covered, and a second consumer would be the trigger for a direct test.
