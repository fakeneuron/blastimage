### Execution plan (guidance, not binding steps)

1. List the landing's scripts, styles, analytics, forms and embeds (as NAT-478 asks).
2. Add nosniff, Referrer-Policy, X-Frame-Options and Permissions-Policy first; these are low risk.
3. Add CSP as Report-Only first, then enforce it. Add HSTS last, after a domain-scope check.
4. Ship through blastimage's normal landing deploy.
5. Checks: `curl -sI https://blastimage.com/` shows 6 of 6; the page renders with no CSP console errors.
